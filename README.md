# Ovia Inc — Data Science

We created this repository as a public starting point for our data science work — the data side of the AI systems we build for business.

Data science is where reliable AI begins. Agents, dashboards, and forecasts are only as good as the data underneath them: how it is collected, cleaned, shaped, and checked. We keep this repo open so the foundation of that work is visible, in plain language, alongside our production services.

**Our main site:** https://oviainc.github.io/ai-services/

## How this fits our work

- **Business intelligence and data** — dashboards, reporting, and data workflows that turn scattered information into decisions. Clean, well-understood data is the first step, and this repo is where we develop that side in the open.
- **AI agents** — agents need grounded, well-structured data to answer, route, and follow through correctly. The data preparation and evaluation habits we build here carry directly into our agent work.
- **Context engineering** — choosing what information reaches a model, in what shape, and at what moment starts with understanding the data itself — its quality, its gaps, and its limits.
- **Machine learning in production** — models moved out of notebooks and into daily operations need reproducible data pipelines and honest reporting, not one-off experiments.
